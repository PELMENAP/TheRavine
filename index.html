The Ravine

Open-source artificial-life sandbox in Unity 6 and C#

The Ravine is an open-source multi-agent artificial-life sandbox (Unity 6, C#, Apache-2.0) where humans and AI agents live in one world under the same rules.

It is an A-Life ecosystem and a testbed for emergent behaviour: colonies of learning agents forage, hunt, communicate, reproduce, evolve and compete in an infinite streamed world. The player is one creature among others, and roles such as castes emerge from adaptation, not from scripts.
